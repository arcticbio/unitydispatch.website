ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

This statement reflects our ongoing commitment to making Unity Dispatch's website accessible to everyone, regardless of disabilities. We are continuously working to enhance the accessibility of our site and improve the user experience for all individuals.

What web accessibility is

Web accessibility ensures that people with disabilities can perceive, understand, navigate, and interact with the web. It encompasses all disabilities that affect access to the web, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

Accessibility adjustments on this site

Unity Dispatch has designed this site in accordance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) [2.0 / 2.1 / 2.2] to ensure it is accessible to the level of [A / AA / AAA]. Our website is compatible with assistive technologies such as screen readers and supports keyboard navigation. Additionally, we have:

    - Used the Accessibility Wizard to identify and address potential accessibility issues
    - Set the language of the site
    - Organized clear heading structures on all pages
    - Provided alternative text to images
    - Implemented color combinations that meet required contrast
    - Reduced the use of motion
    - Ensured accessibility of all multimedia and files on the site

The accessibility of certain pages on our site may depend on contents from third parties. We declare partial compliance with the standard for these pages and are working to improve their accessibility.

Accessibility arrangements in the organization [only add if relevant]

At Unity Dispatch, we are dedicated to accessibility in both our digital presence and physical locations. Our physical offices and branches are equipped with various accessibility arrangements to ensure inclusivity for all visitors.
